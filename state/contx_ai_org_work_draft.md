## Detailed account

At Amadeus Software Labs, I contributed to ContX AI, a hospitality-marketing
application that uses campaign, guest, property, and brand context to generate
personalized email and advertising content. Alongside the application work, I
built a working Pod Brain knowledge-layer prototype to make project and domain
context usable by AI development agents. The design organized information from
Confluence, meeting notes, customer discussions, and engineering artifacts as
structured Markdown and JSON, with links back to authoritative sources.

My proposed knowledge model covered ownership, approval states, indexing, and
the lifecycle of approved, superseded, and deprecated material. I explored
automated ingestion and summarization to keep that context current. I also
ran hands-on language-model and multi-agent evaluations for marketing copy,
structured email content, image generation, and output validation, and joined
data-integration design for hotel offers, availability, rates, property content,
and facilities. Privacy, factual grounding, and human review were part of the
design requirements. The detailed data-integration and guardrail ownership in
this paragraph remains **proposed**, pending separate confirmation. Production
deployment of Pod Brain has not been established.
